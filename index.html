Select the dictionaries you need. Word lookups and suggestions run on your device after installation.

Clear about your data.

No ads or account required. Typed messages are not uploaded and AMK keeps no passive typing history. Downloads require a connection. Android uses Google ML Kit for on-device dictionary explanations; its SDK sends performance and usage metrics to Google. Read the full privacy information.
