Join the Android closed test

The test is available to invited Google accounts. Being added to the invitation list does not enroll you automatically. Google provides both an Android store link and a web enrollment link: eligible accounts may join directly through Play on Android. The web route below is a useful alternative when the store link shows Not found.

  1. In Google Play Store, tap your profile and check which Google account is selected. Give that exact email address to the AMK organizer privately so it can be added to the tester list.
  2. Open the enrollment link in Chrome or your normal browser, using that same Google account. If opening from WhatsApp fails, copy the link into Chrome.
  3. On Google's page, choose the option to become a tester. If you previously joined AMK's internal test, leave that internal test first, then enroll in the closed test.
  4. After Google confirms enrollment, use its Google Play download link. Keep the same account selected in Play Store.

“Item doesn't exist” / “Not found” / “Nicht gefunden”

This message alone does not identify the cause. First check invitation eligibility, enrollment and the selected account in both browser and Play Store. The direct Android store link is valid, but Google must recognize the selected account as eligible and enrolled. If that flow fails, the web page can help confirm eligibility and enrollment.

  • Check that the browser and Play Store use the exact invited Google account.
  • Confirm the enrollment page says you have joined. Simply receiving a link does not confirm enrollment; complete the join step offered by Google Play or the web page.
  • If you changed test tracks, leave the internal test before joining the closed test.
  • After a recent access change, allow time for Google Play to update and try again.
  • If access still fails, send support your phone model, Android version, Play country, exact link and a screenshot of the enrollment result. Send your Google account address privately; never send your password or verification code.

The organizer can then check country targeting, account eligibility and device compatibility. Do not uninstall a working copy to troubleshoot access; uninstalling may erase saved words and settings.

Set up Android

  1. Install or update AMK through the enrolled Google Play account.
  2. Open AMK, choose your languages and download their dictionaries while connected.
  3. Use AMK's keyboard settings shortcut to enable it, then select AMK as your input keyboard. Android shows a standard warning because keyboards can read text in ordinary input fields.
  4. Try AMK in Notes or another ordinary text field. Use the eye button for languages, appearance and Your words.

Android dictionary explanations may need a first Wi-Fi download of Google's translation model. Installed models and dictionaries work offline. The English source remains available while an explanation model is unavailable.

Set up iPhone or iPad

Use your existing TestFlight invitation if one has been provided. AMK's public App Store release is still awaiting review as of 2 November 2026.

  1. Open AMK and choose/download your dictionaries.
  2. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then add AMK.
  3. Allow Full Access is needed to share downloaded dictionaries, favourites and appearance between AMK and its keyboard, and for downloads from keyboard settings. It does not mean AMK uploads typed messages. Disabling it can limit shared features.
  4. In a normal text field, use the globe key to choose AMK. Secure fields use Apple's system keyboard.

Words, favourites and translations

Type a word and select a suggestion. Tap a dictionary translation to replace a completed word; choose another meaning for ambiguous words. Long-press a word to explore its history and tap the star to save it. Word equivalents cover words and short phrases, rather than whole conversations.

Send useful feedback

Include your AMK build, phone/OS, app where the issue occurs, steps, expected result and actual result. For a translation issue, include the word, source/target languages and selected sense. Remove private conversations from screenshots. Android's Improvements feature opens an email draft for you to review and send.

Before a release, testers should check typing/deletion, accents, language switching, translations, favourites, emoji, themes, offline use and update persistence in a notes app and messaging apps. The grey band above the iOS keyboard is still under investigation.
