AMK privacy information

AMK provides an offline keyboard, word suggestions and dictionary translations. Text around the cursor and brief editing state are processed in memory on your device. AMK does not keep a passive typing history or send typed text, messages, personal words or feedback reports to the developer. Suggestions are disabled in Android password fields; iOS uses Apple's secure keyboard in secure fields.

Language dictionaries download separately after selection in AMK settings. Initial setup selects the phone's supported default language, with English fallback. Android has Internet permission for dictionary and translation-model downloads. On iOS, Allow Full Access is needed to share downloaded dictionaries, favourites and appearance settings between the app and keyboard, and to download from keyboard settings. You can disable it in iOS Settings, but doing so can limit those shared features. Installed dictionaries remain usable offline. AMK makes no advertising or account requests. The Android Google ML Kit translation SDK sends performance and usage metrics to Google as described below.

Dictionary requests go to public files hosted by GitHub and its release-download infrastructure. Requests identify the dictionary file and expose normal network information such as IP address to the hosting provider under its privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Typed text and surrounding messages are never included in requests. App updates may provide new versioned dictionaries; downloads verify their checksums before installation.

Language and appearance preferences, personal words and meanings that you explicitly save, favorite words and their dictionary notes, downloaded dictionaries, and translation reports you choose to save remain in private on-device storage. Android app backup is disabled. iOS downloaded dictionaries are excluded from backup. Removing a dictionary does not delete personal vocabulary.

A translation report contains the word, language pair, proposed translation, selected meaning, optional correction, creation time and app version. It does not contain surrounding messages. Android report export uses the document picker: your chosen destination may be a cloud provider governed by its own privacy policy. AMK does not automatically upload reports.

On iOS, favorite words are saved only when you tap the star. Allow Full Access lets the keyboard and containing AMK app share favorites through private on-device App Group storage. Tap the filled star to remove a favorite. Favorites are never uploaded.

You can remove dictionaries in language settings, delete personal words in Your own languages and words, and delete reports in Translation feedback. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-private data. Separately exported files remain at their chosen destination and must be deleted there. Platform distribution and system services follow Google's, Apple's and the device provider's policies.

Android dictionary explanations: bundled editorial notes use the supported phone language immediately. Additional English-source meanings and etymologies can be translated locally with Google Translate through ML Kit. The first model download requires Wi-Fi; installed models work offline. Only dictionary strings enter this translator, never typed messages, personal vocabulary or surrounding text. ML Kit does not upload its input or output, but can contact Google for model updates and sends SDK performance/utilization metrics under Google's privacy policy. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Automatic translations are powered by Google Translate. Google provides no warranties about translation accuracy, reliability, merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose or non-infringement. https://cloud.google.com/translate

Recent emojis are stored only after you insert an emoji in a non-password field. Up to 36 choices are retained privately on this device. Search queries remain in memory and are cleared when a new keyboard session starts. Favorite words are stored only when you select their star, and may be removed in AMK or keyboard settings.

Android Improvements: suggestions you write are kept as a private on-device draft until you clear it or uninstall AMK. Send suggestion opens your email app with your message addressed to the AMK contact; you review and send it there. AMK does not send it automatically. Your email service processes sent messages under its own policies.

Google describes ML Kit diagnostics and usage data as including device/app information, installation identifiers, performance and error information, feature events and configured translation languages. ML Kit uses Firebase installations and remote configuration for diagnostics. Google states that SDK telemetry is encrypted in transit. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure .
